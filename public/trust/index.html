Enterprise Trust Centre

Trust, security & operational assurance.

A practical overview of controls currently represented in the production platform. ÒsánVault does not claim certifications or controls that have not been independently established.

Security architecture

  • Platform-managed authentication and role-based authorization.
  • Tenant/organization isolation for organization-scoped workflows.
  • Administrative access controls for sensitive commercial and governance operations.
  • Audit logging and controlled evidence workflows.
  • Server-side validation and fail-closed handling for protected operations.
  • Private handling for submitted institutional evidence where the workflow requires it.

Evidence & provenance

Source, verification status, authority and provenance are kept distinct from business assertions. Public intelligence is not treated as authoritative merely because a record exists.

Availability & recovery

The production programme includes application health monitoring, backup/recovery readiness and controlled failure-recovery tests. Provider-level disaster-recovery commitments remain subject to the applicable hosting agreement.

Data protection

Personal-data processing is governed by the Privacy Notice and applicable Nigerian data-protection requirements. Enterprise customers may require additional contractual data-processing terms.

Customer security responsibilities

  • Protect account credentials and integration secrets.
  • Use least-privilege roles.
  • Do not upload unlawful or unnecessary sensitive information.
  • Notify ÒsánVault of suspected compromise or misuse.

Security contact

Report security concerns to security@osanvaultafrica.com. Do not include passwords, private keys or unnecessary personal data.
