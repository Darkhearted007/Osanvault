Trust, Security & Operational Assurance

A factual overview of the controls represented in the current application architecture and the boundaries that still require contracts, operational evidence or independent certification.

Certification statement: ÒsánVault does not claim certifications or regulatory approvals that have not been independently established. This page does not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS, FedRAMP, government certification or penetration-testing certification.
IMPLEMENTED / REPRESENTED

Authentication & Authorization

Platform-managed authentication, role-based authorization, protected administrative routes and access-tier enforcement are part of the application architecture.

IMPLEMENTED / REPRESENTED

Tenant & Organization Isolation

Organization-scoped workflows and tenant-aware data paths are part of the platform architecture, with protected administrative surfaces.

IMPLEMENTED / REPRESENTED

Audit Logging

Commercial request review and evidence actions are recorded through existing audit structures; broader platform audit and operational logging are also present.

IMPLEMENTED / REPRESENTED

Evidence & Provenance

Evidence records, source references, content hashes and verification states are separated from ordinary business assertions where those workflows are implemented.

IMPLEMENTED / REPRESENTED

Verification Workflows

Institutional request verification uses defined evidence types, review states and controlled status transitions. Verification is not equivalent to legal title or regulatory approval.

IMPLEMENTED / REPRESENTED

Private Evidence Handling

Commercial evidence can be stored through the existing private storage workflow; public exposure is explicitly separated from evidence submission.

IMPLEMENTED / REPRESENTED

Monitoring & Recovery Readiness

The production programme includes application monitoring, backup/recovery readiness and controlled failure-recovery validation. Provider-specific commitments remain subject to hosting terms.

CONTRACTUAL

Customer-Specific Security Terms

Enterprise agreements may define security schedules, data-processing terms, incident obligations, retention schedules and support responsibilities beyond the public product baseline.

PLANNED / AS APPLICABLE

Security Assessments

Future independent assessments, penetration testing or formal certification may be commissioned as business requirements and evidence warrant. No such certification is claimed here.

REQUIRES EXTERNAL CERTIFICATION

Formal Certifications

Any SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS, FedRAMP or government certification would require independent scope, assessment and evidence. None is represented by this page.

Customer security responsibilities

Protect credentials and integration secrets; use least-privilege access; only submit data necessary for the agreed workflow; and report suspected compromise or misuse.

Operational assurance boundary
