ÒsánVault Africa — Regulatory & Risk Notice

Effective date: 26 August 2026

Technology platform, not a blanket licence

ÒsánVault Africa's software production-readiness certification means the application has passed its internal technical gates. It does not mean OSANVAULT AFRICA LTD. holds every financial, securities, property, mining, payment, data-protection or other regulatory licence that could become relevant to a particular product or transaction.

Digital assets and tokenised real-world assets

Before launching a token, offering fractional interests, accepting investment funds, providing exchange/custody/advisory services or otherwise targeting investors, the company must obtain jurisdiction-specific legal advice and determine whether Nigerian SEC rules, registration, filing or another exemption applies.

Property and minerals

Digital representation does not itself create legal title. Property interests, land rights, mineral rights, licences, permits, surveys and underlying ownership must be independently verified with the relevant authorities and qualified professionals.

Data protection

Personal-data processing must be designed and operated in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable NDPC requirements, including appropriate governance, security, rights handling and any required registration/compliance steps.

Customer due diligence

Where a regulated activity is introduced, the product must implement the applicable KYC/AML, sanctions, transaction-monitoring, recordkeeping and reporting controls before activation.

Launch restriction

Features that could constitute regulated investment, financial, payment, custody, exchange or other licensed activities should remain disabled until the applicable legal and regulatory approval path has been completed.
