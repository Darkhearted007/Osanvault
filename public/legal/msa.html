Enterprise Master Services Agreement

Enterprise templates for negotiation and legal review

This is a commercial drafting framework only. It is not binding merely because it is published and is not legal advice.

Framework topics

  • Parties and authority
  • Services, subscription and pilot/project scope
  • Fees, taxes and payment terms
  • Customer responsibilities and acceptable use
  • Data protection and confidentiality
  • Security
  • Intellectual property, customer data and third-party data
  • Service levels and support
  • Warranties and disclaimers
  • Liability and indemnification where applicable
  • Term, renewal, suspension and termination
  • Data return/deletion and transition assistance
  • Governing law and dispute resolution

Final clauses, schedules and risk allocation must be negotiated and reviewed by qualified counsel before execution.
