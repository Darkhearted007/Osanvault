Enterprise Master Services Agreement

Commercial template for negotiation and legal review · Not a binding agreement by publication

Recommended contract schedule

  1. Parties and authority to contract.
  2. Services, subscription, project or pilot scope.
  3. Fees, invoicing, taxes and payment terms.
  4. Customer responsibilities and acceptable use.
  5. Data protection and DPA incorporation.
  6. Confidentiality and information security.
  7. Intellectual property and permitted data use.
  8. Service levels and support.
  9. Warranties, disclaimers and limitations of liability.
  10. Indemnities where applicable.
  11. Term, renewal, suspension and termination.
  12. Data return/deletion and transition assistance.
  13. Governing law and dispute resolution.
Important: this is a commercial drafting framework. A signed customer MSA must be reviewed by qualified counsel and tailored to the transaction and jurisdiction.
