Data Processing Addendum

Enterprise template for legal review · Not executed by publication

This DPA is intended to document data-processing responsibilities between OSANVAULT AFRICA LTD. and an enterprise customer where one party processes personal data on behalf of the other.

Core terms to finalize

  • Subject matter, duration and documented processing instructions.
  • Categories of personal data and data subjects.
  • Confidentiality and authorized personnel.
  • Technical and organizational security measures.
  • Subprocessor approval and flow-down obligations.
  • International transfers and applicable safeguards.
  • Data-subject rights assistance and incident notification.
  • Deletion/return of data at contract termination.
  • Audit and compliance cooperation.

Legal review

The executed DPA should be tailored to the customer's role, data flows, jurisdictions and applicable Nigerian data-protection requirements before processing begins.
