Data Processing Addendum

Enterprise templates for negotiation and legal review

This is a framework only. It is not binding merely because it is published and is not a finalized contract or legal advice.

Framework

  1. Roles: identify controller/processor roles and any other applicable roles.
  2. Instructions: document processing instructions, purpose, duration and services.
  3. Data: document categories of personal data and data subjects.
  4. Confidentiality: define authorized personnel and confidentiality duties.
  5. Security: record agreed technical and organizational measures.
  6. Subprocessors: define approval/notice and flow-down obligations.
  7. International transfers: identify transfers and applicable safeguards.
  8. Rights: define cooperation with data-subject rights requests.
  9. Incidents: define notification, investigation and cooperation.
  10. Return/deletion: define termination handling, backups and deletion.
  11. Audit/cooperation: define reasonable evidence and cooperation arrangements.
Nigeria: tailor the executed DPA to actual data flows and applicable Nigerian data-protection requirements, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and relevant NDPC requirements. Publication of this page does not itself establish compliance.
