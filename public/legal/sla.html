Service Level Agreement

Enterprise templates for negotiation and legal review

This page is not binding merely because it is published. No uptime percentage is promised here.

Framework

  • Availability target and measurement period
  • Covered services and exclusions
  • Planned maintenance
  • Incident severity and response targets
  • Escalation and communications
  • Recovery objectives
  • Service credits or other remedies where contractually agreed

Final values are negotiated per enterprise agreement and should reflect actual operational capability and defined telemetry.
