Service Level Agreement

Enterprise template for negotiation and legal review · No service level is promised by this page

Items to be agreed per contract

  • Service scope and covered production services.
  • Availability target and measurement window.
  • Planned maintenance and exclusions.
  • Severity definitions and support response targets.
  • Incident communications and escalation contacts.
  • Backup and recovery objectives where contracted.
  • Service credits or other remedies, if agreed.

Operational evidence

Actual SLA reporting should be based on defined production telemetry and the agreed measurement methodology rather than marketing claims.

Enterprise contracts may include a negotiated SLA schedule.
