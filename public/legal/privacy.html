Effective date: 26 August 2026

OSANVAULT AFRICA LTD. takes data protection seriously. This notice explains the categories and purposes of personal-data processing associated with the platform.

1. Data we may process

Depending on the service, this may include identity and contact information, account information, submitted documents, transaction or asset records, technical logs, device/security information and communications.

We may process information to provide the service, authenticate users, protect accounts, maintain audit trails, investigate incidents, perform requested asset workflows, comply with legal obligations and improve platform reliability.

3. Lawful basis and minimisation

Processing should use an applicable lawful basis, collect only information reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, and apply appropriate retention and security controls.

4. Data-subject rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction and other controls over their personal data. Requests may be sent to privacy@osanvaultafrica.com.

5. Security

ÒsánVault uses access controls, tenant isolation, audit logging and operational security measures. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Third parties and transfers

Where service providers or cross-border processing are used, applicable contractual, security and data-protection requirements must be addressed before production use.

7. Regulatory compliance

OSANVAULT AFRICA LTD. will assess and maintain any registrations, filings, data-protection obligations or other regulatory requirements applicable to its actual activities and jurisdictions.

8. Contact

Privacy enquiries: privacy@osanvaultafrica.com.

This notice is a product-level privacy framework and should be reviewed and approved by qualified Nigerian privacy counsel/DPO before relying on it as the company's final legal notice.
