Data Retention & Deletion

Product-level framework · Effective 19 September 2026

ÒsánVault retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the service, security, audit, contractual and legal purposes applicable to the record.

Retention categories

  • Account and organization records: according to service and legal requirements.
  • Evidence and verification records: retained according to the applicable verification and contractual lifecycle.
  • Security and audit records: retained for security, investigation and governance requirements.
  • Commercial records: retained for contractual, accounting and dispute-resolution requirements.

Deletion

Deletion requests are handled subject to legal retention duties, legitimate security/audit requirements and contractual obligations. Customers may receive additional retention schedules in their enterprise agreement or DPA.
