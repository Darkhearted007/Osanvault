ÒsánVault Africa

An evidence-backed national asset and territory intelligence platform that helps institutions turn fragmented asset, GIS and provenance signals into decision-ready intelligence.

The problem

Institutions working across African territories often face fragmented asset records, inconsistent spatial context, disconnected evidence and slow verification workflows. ÒsánVault is designed to make those signals usable in one auditable workflow.

The solution

Institution → Asset → Evidence → GIS → Intelligence → Decision

  • Asset onboarding and verification workflows
  • GIS and territory intelligence
  • Evidence and provenance controls
  • Decision-oriented asset intelligence
  • Audit and operational readiness controls

Business model

ÒsánVault monetizes the intelligence infrastructure behind institutional asset decisions. Developers use the platform to screen opportunities, structure project evidence and reduce diligence friction; banks, funds and other institutions use it to assess and monitor asset or project exposure; asset owners can structure underused assets for development and partnership conversations.

Customer value
  • Faster opportunity discovery and screening
  • Lower information and diligence friction
  • Better evidence, provenance and GIS context
  • Structured institutional review and monitoring
ÒsánVault revenue
  • Explorer subscriptions
  • Enterprise annual licences
  • Project / asset intelligence services
  • Enterprise API and data access
  • Custom institutional intelligence programmes

Initial commercial motion remains non-regulated Explorer and Enterprise access, controlled onboarding, workspace provisioning and institution-specific intelligence workflows. The platform does not activate regulated investment, custody, exchange or regulated payment functionality without the applicable legal/regulatory pathway.

Why now

Africa's physical assets and territories generate enormous operational information, but decision-makers need a trusted intelligence layer that connects location, evidence, provenance and institutional workflows rather than another disconnected data register.

Go-to-market
