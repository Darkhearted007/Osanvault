Continental Jurisdiction Framework

A country-aware governance layer for continental growth. The Group constitution remains common; each jurisdiction receives its own legal, regulatory, ownership, land, asset, tax and community-rights profile before operational entry.

One Continental Architecture. Many Local Legal Realities.

Continental expansion does not mean legal uniformity. Every country must be admitted through evidence-backed jurisdiction review, local-law analysis and an accession decision. The Group framework coordinates; local law governs.
