Institutional Safeguards

Indigenous & Community Rights

Where legitimate indigenous or community rights are implicated, the platform requires a documented rights review before ownership restructuring, transfer, dilution or encumbrance. This is a governance safeguard, not a substitute for applicable law, customary-rights processes or legal advice.

Beneficial Ownership

PSC and beneficial-ownership status remains explicitly reviewable. The platform will not label an ownership chain legally complete without appropriate evidence.

Founder / Control Protection

Change-of-control, major issuance and transfer events are treated as reserved matters subject to the constitution, shareholder arrangements and applicable law.

Control principle: this register is an administrative governance system. It does not itself create, transfer or extinguish legal ownership. Any executed transaction must be supported by the required instruments, approvals, statutory registers and filings.
