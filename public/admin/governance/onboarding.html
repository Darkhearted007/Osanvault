Asset Onboarding & Evidence Verification

A controlled intake and verification workflow for turning a proposed land or strategic asset into an evidence-backed Asset Passport without allowing an unverified assertion to become an institutional fact.

Verification Doctrine

Every material assertion must have a source, a verification status, an accountable reviewer and a recorded decision.

Safe-haven rule: community, indigenous, customary and third-party claims remain visible throughout the workflow. A claim cannot be silently discarded because it conflicts with a commercial submission.
