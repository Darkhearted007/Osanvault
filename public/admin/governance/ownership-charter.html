ÒsánVault Africa Ltd.

Indigenous & Founding Ownership Charter

A proposed institutional charter protecting legitimate founding interests, indigenous and community rights, cultural stewardship and fair participation while allowing the Group to expand across African jurisdictions. It is deliberately structured as a governance framework until qualified counsel and the relevant communities, shareholders and authorities convert applicable provisions into legally effective instruments.

Foundational Covenant

Growth must not become dispossession. Where ÒsánVault operates around land, community assets, cultural heritage or documented founding interests, expansion should preserve legitimate rights, transparent benefit pathways, due process and locally lawful consent.

Legal boundary: this charter does not itself establish customary title, land ownership, shareholder rights, trust rights or statutory consent. Each protected interest must be evidenced and validated under the law applicable to the relevant asset and jurisdiction.
