Core Governance Principles

Separation of capacities. Shareholder, director, officer and employee capacities are recorded distinctly.

Documented authority. Material authority should derive from a defined role and approved delegation.

Decision traceability. Material corporate decisions should be recorded through resolutions or other controlled records.

Document control. Governance documents carry an owner, version, status and effective date.
