Stakeholder, Ownership & Beneficial Interest Register

A jurisdiction-aware institutional register for shareholders, persons with significant control, directors, community interests, indigenous/customary interests, institutional partners and other legally or contractually recognised interests.

Continental design principle

Record the interest without assuming the law.

The register distinguishes corporate ownership from community, customary, contractual and other recognised interests. A record is not itself proof of title or ownership; verification depends on applicable law, corporate records, agreements and supporting evidence in the relevant jurisdiction.
