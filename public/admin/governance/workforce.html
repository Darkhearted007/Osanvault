Design Principles

Authority follows office

Holding a job title does not automatically confer legal or financial authority. Delegated authority must be explicitly documented.

Equity follows approved instruments

Employee equity eligibility is separate from an actual grant. Grants require the approved plan, required approvals, instruments and applicable legal/tax review.

Continuity is institutional

Critical positions receive succession planning so the organization does not depend on a single individual for governance, compliance or operational continuity.

Exit is a control event

Leavers trigger access, authority, asset, document and equity checks rather than simply changing an employment status field.

Institutional safeguard: the position catalogue is a planning and governance structure. It does not create employment, appointment, delegated authority, compensation or equity rights until the appropriate documents and approvals exist.
