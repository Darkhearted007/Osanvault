Authority & Delegation Matrix

A controlled register defining which organisational roles may make, approve, sign or recommend decisions, subject to limits, conditions and secondary approvals.

Control Principle

Authority follows role—not personality.

Delegations are role-based, time-bound where necessary, and subject to defined financial and operational controls. The matrix does not itself confer statutory powers that must arise from the company's constitution, board authority or applicable law.
