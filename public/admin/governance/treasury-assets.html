Capital, Treasury & Asset Stewardship

The institutional economic-control layer connecting capital, treasury, asset custody, valuation, encumbrances, transactions, stewardship and distribution policy. The registry records verified evidence; it does not create title or ownership by data entry.

Stewardship Covenant

Capital and assets are held under documented authority, evidence and stewardship obligations—not merely recorded as numbers.

Evidence gate: legal title, beneficial interest, community rights, land rights, mineral rights, carbon rights and other asset claims must be supported by appropriate instruments and independently reviewed before the platform treats them as verified.
