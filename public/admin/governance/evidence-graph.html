Asset Evidence Graph

The provenance layer connects people, communities, companies, assets, titles, licences, authorities, documents, claims and verification events so that institutional decisions can be traced to evidence rather than isolated records.

Evidence Doctrine

An assertion is not an institutional fact until its provenance, source, verification status and confidence can be traced.

Conflict rule: contradictory ownership, title, rights or regulatory assertions remain visible and generate a conflict record. High and Critical unresolved conflicts block Asset Passport integrity.
