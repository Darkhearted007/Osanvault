Institutional Integrity Covenant

Growth is acceptable; unmanaged institutional risk is not. The platform separates policy, evidence, assessment, investigation and remediation so governance decisions can be challenged and corrected.

Jurisdiction rule: the Group framework is not a substitute for local legal advice, licences or regulatory determinations. Each country and regulated activity must carry its own obligation and evidence record before being treated as compliant.
