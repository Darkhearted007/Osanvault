Group Constitution & Foundational Charter

A continental governance charter designed to provide common institutional principles while preserving the supremacy of mandatory local law and the separate legal identity of country entities and asset vehicles.

Constitutional principle

Stewardship before extraction. Evidence before representation. Local legitimacy before expansion.

Protected rights are not an operational afterthought. Land, mineral, carbon, cultural and community-linked projects must pass jurisdiction-specific rights and legal review before they are represented as cleared or commercially deployable. Nothing in this Charter extinguishes rights created by applicable law.
