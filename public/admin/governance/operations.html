Institutional Workflow

1. Convene

Record the governing body, agenda, date, chair and secretary.

2. Establish quorum

Record expected and present members before substantive decisions.

3. Declare conflicts

Capture conflicts and recusals before voting or approval.

4. Decide

Connect the decision to its resolution and approval authority.

5. Record & execute

Preserve the decision, actions, owners, deadlines and audit history.

This centre records internal governance activity. It does not itself constitute a legal appointment, statutory filing, shareholder approval or board resolution until the appropriate authority has completed the required process.
