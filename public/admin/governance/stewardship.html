Indigenous & Community Stewardship

An operational rights-protection layer beneath Article III of the Group Constitution. It records community identity, rights claims, consultation, consent where required, benefit-sharing, grievances, safeguards, independent review, clearance decisions and evidence.

Safe Haven Principle

Community and indigenous rights are treated as institutional rights to be identified, evidenced, reviewed and protected — not merely as a due-diligence checkbox.

Critical boundary: this system does not decide whether a community possesses a legal or customary right. It records claims and evidence, routes them through appropriate review, and prevents the platform from representing a project as cleared without the required evidence and approvals.
