Safeguards
Governance principle. The parent constitution establishes the institutional baseline. Country subsidiaries, SPVs and regional entities may adopt local instruments only where they remain consistent with the parent framework, applicable law and protected indigenous/community rights. No register on this dashboard by itself substitutes for a signed legal instrument, statutory filing, board approval or shareholder resolution.
