Constitutional Institutions & Oversight

The institutional layer that turns the Group constitution into functioning boards, committees, delegated authority, conflict controls, reserved matters, resolutions and evidence.

Institutional Principle

Authority must be constituted, bounded, reviewable and evidenced. No officer, committee or subsidiary board receives authority merely because it exists in the hierarchy; authority is defined by mandate, delegation and applicable law.

Independence & rights gate: material conflicts require disclosure and recusal. Protected/community-linked asset decisions require stewardship review and, where material, independent review.
