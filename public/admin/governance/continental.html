Continental Jurisdiction & Country Governance

A country-by-country governance architecture for a continental group. Each jurisdiction has its own legal, ownership, regulatory, land, tax, rights, investment and country-risk layer while remaining connected to Group constitutional standards.

Continental Governance Covenant

One institutional standard; many sovereign legal environments. Group governance provides the common control framework. Local law, title, regulation, customary rights and regulatory evidence remain jurisdiction-specific and cannot be replaced by Group records.

Evidence gate: country records are planning/research structures until authoritative local instruments and professional legal review establish their applicable requirements.
