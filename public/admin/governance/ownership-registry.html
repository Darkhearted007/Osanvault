Ownership & Beneficial Ownership Registry

A controlled ownership register separating legal title, beneficial ownership, economic participation and voting rights. The registry is evidence-driven: no person, community, ownership percentage or protected right is invented without a supporting instrument or verification record.

Ownership Integrity Principle

Do not confuse legal title with beneficial interest, economic participation, voting power or protected community rights. Each category is recorded separately so future transactions can be evaluated without collapsing distinct legal relationships.

Verification gate: participant percentages are intentionally empty until supported by current company records, shareholder instruments, beneficial ownership filings, trusts or other legally relevant evidence. This registry is an internal governance layer and does not itself create ownership rights.
