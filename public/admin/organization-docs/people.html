People & HR Handbook

This framework supports a professional, accountable and secure workforce. Employment terms must comply with applicable law and individual contracts.

People lifecycle

  1. Workforce plan and approved role.
  2. Recruitment and candidate assessment.
  3. Reference/background checks where lawful and appropriate.
  4. Offer and employment documentation.
  5. Structured onboarding and access provisioning.
  6. Performance management and development.
  7. Offboarding, access removal and asset return.

Conduct

  • Act honestly and in the company's legitimate interests.
  • Protect confidential information and personal data.
  • Disclose conflicts of interest.
  • No bribery, fraud, harassment, retaliation or deliberate evidence manipulation.
  • Use company systems only for authorized purposes.

Performance

Every role should have an accountable manager, defined responsibilities and measurable objectives appropriate to the role. Performance records should be factual, fair and appropriately confidential.

Offboarding

  • Disable system and administrative access promptly.
  • Recover devices, keys, documents and other company property.
  • Transfer active work and records.
  • Confirm confidentiality and continuing obligations.
  • Record completion of the offboarding checklist.
