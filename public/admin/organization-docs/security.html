Information Security & Privacy Standard

Principle: Access to organizational, personal, financial and asset evidence must be limited to legitimate purpose and authorized roles.

Identity & access

  • Use platform-managed authentication and role-based access controls.
  • Apply least privilege.
  • Separate administrative privileges from ordinary user access.
  • Review privileged access periodically and after role changes.
  • Remove access promptly at offboarding.

Evidence integrity

  • Record source, timestamp and provenance where available.
  • Do not overwrite authoritative evidence without preserving history.
  • Reviewer identity and decision must be traceable.
  • Conflicting evidence must remain visible rather than silently discarded.

Privacy

  • Collect only data reasonably necessary for the stated purpose.
  • Provide appropriate privacy notices and lawful basis where required.
  • Restrict access to personal data.
  • Maintain retention/deletion rules appropriate to the data category and legal obligation.
  • Use approved processors/vendors and contractual safeguards where required.

Incident response

Suspected compromise, unauthorized access, data loss, credential exposure or evidence tampering must be escalated promptly to the designated security/management owner. Preserve logs and evidence, contain the incident, assess impact, notify required parties where legally necessary and document lessons learned.
