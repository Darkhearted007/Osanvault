Finance & Treasury Manual

Important: This manual intentionally does not invent budgets, balances, valuations, bank accounts or financial thresholds.

Budget control

  • Maintain an approved annual operating and capital budget.
  • Assign every material spend to a budget owner.
  • Require variance review for material deviations.
  • Do not represent proposed or budgeted funds as cash held.

Procure-to-pay

  1. Approved business need.
  2. Vendor due diligence and conflict check.
  3. Quote/selection evidence appropriate to the spend.
  4. Purchase approval and contract where required.
  5. Receipt/service confirmation.
  6. Invoice validation.
  7. Authorized payment.
  8. Reconciliation and record retention.

Treasury

  • Separate operating, client/segregated and restricted funds where legally required.
  • Maintain authorized signatories.
  • Reconcile bank and payment accounts regularly.
  • Monitor liquidity and obligations.
  • Never commingle client or trust funds with company operating funds where segregation is required.

Reporting

Management reporting should distinguish actuals, commitments, approved budgets, forecasts and externally verified figures. Material financial reporting requires appropriate review before institutional distribution.
