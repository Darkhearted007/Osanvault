Delegation & Approval Matrix

Working authority framework. Monetary thresholds must be populated by the Board after reviewing capitalization, cash flow, banking arrangements and applicable corporate approvals.

Do not infer authority from job title alone. Until a written delegation is approved, the higher approval level applies.

Segregation of duties

  • Requester should not be sole approver.
  • Approver should not be sole payment executor.
  • Asset capture should be separated from final verification.
  • Evidence changes require traceability and reviewer identity.
  • Administrative access should be limited and reviewed.
