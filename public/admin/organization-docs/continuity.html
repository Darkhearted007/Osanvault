Business Continuity & Incident Plan

Framework for maintaining critical organizational functions during technology, security, operational, supplier or external disruption.

Incident levels

  • Critical: material loss of confidentiality/integrity/availability, major financial/legal exposure or threat to critical operations.
  • High: significant service degradation, security event or operational failure requiring coordinated response.
  • Moderate: localized issue manageable by the owning function with escalation if unresolved.
  • Low: routine defect or service issue handled through normal operations.

Response sequence

  1. Detect and record.
  2. Assign incident owner.
  3. Contain immediate harm.
  4. Preserve logs/evidence.
  5. Assess impact and affected stakeholders.
  6. Escalate to management, legal, security or regulators where required.
  7. Recover services safely.
  8. Conduct post-incident review and track corrective actions.

Critical assets to protect

  • Identity and privileged access.
  • Asset/evidence records.
  • Financial and payment records.
  • Corporate/legal records.
  • Production applications and databases.
  • Third-party credentials and integrations.
  • Customer/institutional communications.

Continuity readiness

Maintain tested backups, documented recovery ownership, dependency maps, vendor contacts and emergency communications. Recovery objectives should be approved based on business criticality rather than invented after an incident.
