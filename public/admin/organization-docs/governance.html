Corporate Governance Manual

This manual establishes the intended governance architecture of ÒsánVault Africa Ltd. It is designed to preserve accountability as the organization grows from an operating company into a broader African asset-infrastructure group.

Legal control: Where this framework conflicts with the company's filed constitutional documents, shareholder agreements, applicable law or a valid board resolution, the legally controlling instrument prevails.

1. Governance layers

  • Shareholders: ownership rights and matters reserved by law or constitutional documents.
  • Board: strategy, fiduciary oversight, risk appetite, executive accountability and reserved matters.
  • Executive Management: day-to-day execution within approved strategy, budgets and delegated authority.
  • Assurance functions: risk, compliance, audit and legal review should retain appropriate independence from activities they assure.
  • Subsidiaries/SPVs: ring-fenced legal, asset and financial responsibilities where required.

2. Board responsibilities

  • Approve strategy, annual plans, material budgets and major changes to business model.
  • Oversee financial integrity, risk management, compliance and protection of company assets.
  • Appoint, evaluate and where necessary replace executive leadership.
  • Approve related-party transactions and other reserved matters.
  • Ensure material decisions are documented through minutes or resolutions.

3. Committee framework

As scale requires, the Board may establish Finance/Audit, Risk & Compliance, Investment/Asset, Technology/Security and People/Remuneration committees. Committee mandates must define authority, membership, quorum, reporting and conflicts.

4. Meetings & records

  • Maintain agendas, attendance, conflicts, papers, decisions and minutes.
  • Track action owners and due dates.
  • Store authoritative records in the controlled corporate repository.
  • Material resolutions should identify the decision, authority, date and approving body.

5. Conflicts & integrity

Directors, officers and staff must disclose actual, potential or perceived conflicts. A conflicted person should not influence a decision where independent handling is required. Gifts, inducements, bribery and undisclosed related-party benefits are prohibited.

6. Institutional principles

  • Evidence before assertion.
  • Separation of capture, verification and approval.
  • No self-approval of material transactions.
  • Auditability of material decisions.
  • Protection of community and stakeholder interests where applicable.
  • Legal and regulatory review before entering regulated activities.

Owner: Board/Executive Management · Review: annual or on material change
