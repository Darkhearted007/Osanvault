Risk, Compliance & Ethics Framework

Regulatory boundary: ÒsánVault must not launch a regulated financial, securities, payments, custody, investment or digital-asset activity solely because a product workflow exists. Each activity requires jurisdiction-specific legal and regulatory analysis.

Risk taxonomy

  • Legal and regulatory risk
  • Asset/title/evidence risk
  • Financial and liquidity risk
  • Fraud, corruption and sanctions risk
  • Technology and cybersecurity risk
  • Data/privacy risk
  • Operational and vendor risk
  • Reputational and stakeholder risk
  • Climate/environmental and community risk where relevant

Compliance controls

  • Maintain a compliance obligations register by jurisdiction.
  • Track licences, registrations, filings and renewal dates where applicable.
  • Perform KYC/KYB/AML controls where legally required.
  • Screen relevant counterparties and transactions against applicable sanctions requirements.
  • Maintain conflicts and related-party registers.
  • Escalate suspected fraud, bribery, misconduct or material control failure.

Risk assessment

Every material risk should identify likelihood, impact, owner, controls, residual exposure, treatment and review date. Risk scores must be based on an approved methodology; the dashboard must not fabricate scores when underlying inputs are absent.

Whistleblowing & ethics

Provide a confidential reporting channel, prohibit retaliation, preserve evidence and route allegations to an independent reviewer where appropriate. Investigations should be documented and handled on a need-to-know basis.
