Operating Procedures Manual

Core operating controls for a trusted African real-world-asset infrastructure company.

1. Asset capture

  1. Intake: identify owner/submitter, asset category, location and purpose.
  2. Evidence: collect source documents and record provenance.
  3. Classification: separate facts, evidence, inference and assumptions.
  4. Conflict check: identify duplicate, contradictory or stale information.
  5. Review: route to the appropriate verifier.

2. Verification

Verification must identify the source, reviewer, date, scope and limitations. A failed or incomplete check must not be represented as verified. Verification is a decision with an audit trail, not a visual badge.

3. Institutional onboarding

  1. Collect organization identity and authorized representative information.
  2. Perform appropriate KYC/KYB and sanctions screening where required.
  3. Confirm role, permissions and purpose of access.
  4. Accept applicable terms/confidentiality instruments.
  5. Record approval and provisioning.

4. Project lifecycle

Opportunity → screening → evidence → legal/title review → technical/commercial assessment → risk review → investment/underwriting decision → approval → controlled execution → monitoring → closeout.

5. Change control

Material production changes should have an owner, reason, testing evidence, rollback consideration and deployment record. Emergency changes require retrospective review.

6. Records

Authoritative records must be attributable, protected from unauthorized alteration, retrievable and retained according to the applicable records schedule.
