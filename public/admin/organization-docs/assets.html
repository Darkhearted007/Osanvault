Asset Due-Diligence & Evidence Standard

Core rule: Asset capture is not verification. Asset data must move through explicit evidence, verification, intelligence and capital-readiness gates.

Asset passport minimum

  • Unique asset identifier.
  • Asset type and description.
  • Location/geographic reference where appropriate.
  • Claimed owner or rights holder, subject to verification.
  • Evidence inventory and provenance.
  • Verification status and reviewer.
  • Known restrictions, disputes or gaps.
  • Valuation status and valuation source where applicable.
  • Economic/project status.
  • Last reviewed date.

Verification gates

  1. Identity: establish the relevant person/entity and authority to submit.
  2. Legal/title: review title, tenure, rights and encumbrances as applicable.
  3. Technical: assess surveys, condition, capacity or technical claims as relevant.
  4. Commercial: assess market, contracts, revenue assumptions and counterparties.
  5. Risk: document material legal, financial, operational, environmental and stakeholder risks.
  6. Investment: only after required evidence and approvals may an opportunity be represented as capital-ready.

Prohibited shortcuts

  • Do not call an asset verified because a document was uploaded.
  • Do not convert inferred ownership into legal ownership.
  • Do not fabricate valuations, market prices, investor interest or transaction status.
  • Do not suppress adverse evidence to improve an asset's score.
