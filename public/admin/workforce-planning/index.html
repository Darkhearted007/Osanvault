Control Framework

1. Approved position
A person cannot be assigned to an undefined corporate position.
2. Appointment scope
Each appointment carries a defined title, scope, status and effective date.
3. Reporting line
Every appointment can be linked to the position it reports to.
4. Workforce record
The assignment becomes part of the authoritative employee master record.

Institutional distinction

Shareholder, director, officer and employee are different capacities. This module therefore records the employment/appointment relationship separately from ownership and corporate directorship. That separation will make future CAC filings, board records and HR records materially cleaner.
